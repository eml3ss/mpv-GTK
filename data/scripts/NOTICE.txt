The GTK adaptation replaces the original OSC/ASS presentation with native GTK
controls and status. Keyframe scanning, boundary validation, lossless FFmpeg
export, file identity checks, backup/rollback, and opening the result retain the
original backend implementation. Configuration is isolated to the player folder.
