Bundled mpv GTK interface icons

These SVGs are a small selection from the Papirus icon theme, maintained by
the Papirus Development Team and contributors:
https://github.com/PapirusDevelopmentTeam/papirus-icon-theme

Papirus is distributed under the GNU General Public License, version 3.
The complete license text is included in LICENSE.GPL3.

The SVG source files are copied unchanged from the Papirus-Dark theme's
symbolic icons and Papirus's multimedia-video-player application icon.
Symbolic links have been resolved into standalone SVG files. SOURCES.tsv
records each original path within Papirus and the copied file's SHA-256.
The resource alias io.github.emless.mpvgtk uses the same application SVG.

Only these files are bundled. Standard internal GTK icons remain supplied
by GTK's own built-in resources and retain their upstream licensing.
